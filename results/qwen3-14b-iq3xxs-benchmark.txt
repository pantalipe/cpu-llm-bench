Baseline for comparison — same underlying model (Qwen3-14B), Q3_K_XL quant, via Ollama/llama-swap:
1.2 tok/s average (see qwen3-14b-baseline.json)

Conclusion: no meaningful speed difference between IQ3_XXS and Q3_K_XL on this CPU. I-quant kernels
in llama.cpp are optimized for AVX2 dot-product instructions; without AVX2 the extra dequant overhead
of the I-quant format isn't offset by any SIMD win, so throughput lands at parity with (or slightly
below) a conventional K-quant at a similar bit-depth. The IQ format's real benefit here is a smaller
file size, not speed.
